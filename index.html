02 / ABOUT

THE ARTIST behind THE LENS

SELF PORTRAIT 2026

Based in Mississauga, I have spent over a decade refining my craft—documenting weddings, photographing editorial campaigns, and capturing portraits that speak louder than words. My work has been featured in Vogue, Elle, National Geographic, and numerous international galleries.

Every frame I compose is a meditation on light and shadow, contrast and composition. I believe the most powerful photographs are not seen—they are felt. They linger in memory like half-remembered dreams.
