THE ARTIST behind THE LENS

SELF PORTRAIT 2026

Hi, I'm Sanjay.

Based in Mississauga, I have spent over five years sharpening my eye for composition, lighting, and visual storytelling. Bringing hands-on experience from event photography alongside my leadership background as a former university photography club president, I approach every shoot with intentionality, passion, and precision.

Photography is a process of continuous evolution—I spend every day experimenting with new techniques, studying light, and refining my work so that every frame is better than the last. My focus is simple: to capture authentic moments and turn them into lasting visual memories.
